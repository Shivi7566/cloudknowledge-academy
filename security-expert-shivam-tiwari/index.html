BACKGROUND & PHILOSOPHY

Engineering Identity at Scale

Throughout my career as an Identity and Access Management engineer, I have architected and deployed enterprise-scale identity solutions spanning Microsoft Entra ID (Azure Active Directory), hybrid Active Directory forests, multi-cloud federation, and automated lifecycle workflows.

Real-world security incidents rarely stem from a lack of tooling; they stem from subtle misconfigurations in Conditional Access policies, forgotten legacy authentication endpoints, over-privileged permanent roles, or misaligned federation claims.

Through CloudKnowledge, I document reproducible architecture blueprints, troubleshooting methodologies, and hands-on lab exercises that teach engineers how production IAM works under the hood.
