Microsoft Identity and Access Administrator

Microsoft Certified: Identity and Access Administrator Associate

Questions
40–60 *
Duration
100 min
Passing score
700 / 1000
Price (USD)
$165 *
Validity
12 months; free online renewal

What this exam is like

Who it's for

Admins and engineers who run Microsoft Entra ID day to day. The core IAM certification.

Prerequisites

None formally. SC-900 or 6 months of Entra ID hands-on recommended.

What it tests

Implement and manage user identities20-25%
Implement authentication and access management25-30%
Plan and implement workload identities20-25%
Plan and automate identity governance20-25%

Prep roadmap

  1. 1
    Set up a lab tenantWeek 1

    Microsoft 365 developer or trial tenant with Entra ID P2 so you can use PIM, ID Protection and Governance.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  2. 2
    Users, groups & hybridWeek 1–2

    Bulk users, dynamic groups, B2B guests, and Entra Connect / Cloud Sync with a small on-prem AD.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  3. 3
    Authentication & Conditional AccessWeek 3–4

    MFA methods, authentication strengths, CA baseline, SSPR, passwordless. The biggest domain.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  4. 4
    Workload identities & appsWeek 5

    App registrations, enterprise apps, SAML/OIDC SSO, managed identities, app consent.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  5. 5
    Governance & PIMWeek 6

    Access packages, access reviews, lifecycle workflows, PIM for roles and groups.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  6. 6
    Practice & reviseWeek 7–8

    Practice assessment, then revisit weak domains in the lab — not just in notes.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  7. 7
    Book and sit the exam

    Schedule through Microsoft Learn with Pearson VUE — online proctored from home or at a test centre. Take the free Microsoft Learn practice assessment first.

    ▶ video✎ blog
  8. ✓
    Certified 🎉

    Add it to LinkedIn and your CV — then pick the next step on the certification path.

  • ✓Run Entra ID for an organisation end to end
  • ✓Design and roll out a Conditional Access baseline
