How to get certified

  1. 01
    Pick your area and platform

    IAM, security, cloud or endpoint — then the platform your company uses (Entra ID, Okta, CyberArk, SailPoint…).

  2. 02
    Read the official skills outline

    Every objective can be asked. Use the domain weights to plan your time.

  3. 03
    Do the labs, not just videos

    Build each objective in a lab tenant or free cloud account. Okta exams are fully hands-on.

  4. 04
    Practise under exam conditions

    Timed practice tests. Aim for 80%+ consistently before you book.

  5. 05
    Book the exam

    Online proctored from home or at a test centre (Pearson VUE for Microsoft, AWS, CompTIA, ISC2).

  6. 06
    Renew and climb the next step

    Microsoft role-based certs renew free every year; AWS and CompTIA every 3 years; Okta every 2.

FAQ

Should I learn Entra ID, Okta, CyberArk or SailPoint?+

Pick the platform your company (or the jobs you're applying for) uses. They are separate tracks — you don't need all of them. Entra ID and Okta are access management (SSO, MFA); CyberArk is privileged access (PAM); SailPoint is identity governance (IGA). Many IAM engineers know one access platform plus one of PAM or IGA.

Which certification should I do first for IAM?+

On Microsoft: SC-900 if you're new, else straight to SC-300. On Okta: Okta Certified Professional. On SailPoint: the free Identity Security Leader. On CyberArk: Defender – PAM.

SC-900 → SC-300 → SC-200 → SC-100: is that the right order?+

Yes for Microsoft Security. SC-100 needs one of SC-300, SC-200 or SC-500 first, so the associate step is mandatory.

AZ-500 is retired — what now?+

AZ-500 retired on Aug 31, 2026. Its replacement is SC-500 (Cloud and AI Security Engineer Associate).

Do I need a paid subscription for labs?+

Mostly no. A Microsoft 365 trial tenant, the Azure free account, the AWS Free Tier and a free Okta org cover most labs.
