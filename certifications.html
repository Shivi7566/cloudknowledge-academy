🎓 Certification Blueprint & Strategic Progression

A proven progression roadmap detailing which certification to take first, prerequisites, domain weightage, and hands-on mapping to prepare for top-tier enterprise roles.

Strategic Progression Order

Which Certification Should You Take First?

01.
Foundational Base: Start with SC-900 (or AZ-900). Covers core security principles, cloud compliance, and Zero Trust concepts. (1-2 months)
02.
Core Identity Specialist (HIGHEST ROI): Take SC-300 (Microsoft Identity & Access Administrator). This qualifies you for 80% of enterprise IAM engineering jobs. Pair it with Okta Certified Professional. (3-4 months)
03.
Privilege & Endpoint Mastery: Proceed to CyberArk Trustee/Defender (PAM) and MD-102 (Intune Endpoint). PAM engineers command premium salaries in enterprise banking and healthcare. (3-5 months)
04.
Master Architect (Capstone): Take SC-100 (Cybersecurity Architect) + AZ-500. Unifies identity, threat protection, data governance, and Zero Trust cloud infrastructure.
